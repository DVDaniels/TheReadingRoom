Follow

Following a site means its new posts come to you, in a reader app you choose. No account, no algorithm, nothing in between. It’s how the open web has always worked, through a free standard called RSS.

Open in The Reading Room Already have The Reading Room? It asks before it adds anything.

New to this? Start with a free reader.

A reader is an app that gathers the sites you follow in one place, newest first. The Reading Room is one, free for Mac, iPhone and iPad: get it on the App Store, then come back to this page and press Open in The Reading Room. (Opening sites from a link needs version 1.2.4 or later, in the beta now.) More about RSS.
