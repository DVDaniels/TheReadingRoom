How it works

For your readers

The button opens a page on readingroomapp.com for your site. With The Reading Room on their Mac, iPhone or iPad, they press Open in The Reading Room and the app asks whether to follow you. Nothing is added unless they say yes. Without the app, the page offers it free on the App Store, and gives your address to copy into any other reader. Opening sites from a link needs version 1.2.4 or later, in the beta now.

Your privacy, and theirs

The follow page has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and loads nothing from other sites. It’s a plain page on readingroomapp.com, hosted by GitHub Pages, and your site’s address simply travels in the link. There’s nothing to sign up for and no list of who follows whom.

Linking straight to the app

If your readers already use The Reading Room, you can skip the page and link straight to the app with readingroom://subscribe?url= followed by your feed address. For visitors without the app, that link does nothing, so the button above is the better choice for most sites.
