Thanks for testing The Reading Room! This build (1.2.7) adds YouTube video descriptions and fixes how notes about a post look on Bluesky and Mastodon. It has everything from 1.2.6 too.

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTIONS
• Open a YouTube video from a channel you follow: its description now shows under the player, with its paragraphs and line breaks.
• Web addresses in the description are links. Tap one to open it.
• Tap a chapter time (like 1:23) to play the video from that point.
• Videos already in the app get their description at the next refresh.

NOTES ABOUT A POST, ON BLUESKY AND MASTODON
• When you write a note about someone's post and share copies, the copy now shows their post (title, site and picture) instead of a second copy of your own words, with the link to your note underneath.

VIDEOS
• Your channels: a row at the top of Videos with every channel you follow, its newest video and how many you haven't watched. Tap one to see only its videos; tap it again (or Show every channel) for all.
• Tap the star on a channel to make it a favorite. Favorites stay first, and also show in the sidebar under Videos, under a Favorite channels heading you can fold away. The arrow beside Videos folds them into Videos. One tap opens just that channel.
• On the Mac, the empty side now says Pick a video to watch it here (and Pick an email in Email).
• The bar now speaks of watching: Unwatched, All videos, Mark all watched, Search videos. Free to read is gone from Videos.

Please tell us if a description looks wrong, or if tapping a chapter time doesn't start the video where it should (Mac and iPhone/iPad).
• A channel or blog that posts rarely could look empty when you follow lots of busy feeds (the app only loaded the newest 2,000 stories overall). Now every feed's newest 20 always show.

CONVERSATIONS
• Mark all as read is now at the top, next to Since you were here. On Everything it clears the Conversations number in the sidebar.
• The Conversations chip counts only conversations you haven't read.
• If Mastodon won't favorite or follow, the app now says why, and only asks you to reconnect when the permission is really missing. Tap a red message to clear it.

MARKETS ON TODAY
• Settings › Markets on Today: up to five stocks or funds and five coins at the top of Today, with the day's change. Crypto works right away (CoinGecko). Stocks need a free Finnhub key (finnhub.io, no credit card), kept in your Keychain. Prices may be delayed.

SHARE WHAT YOU READ
• Quote cards: select words in a story and tap Quote card (or Quote card under any highlight). You get a picture of the passage with the writer's name and site, to send in WhatsApp, Messages or anywhere. Paper or Night.
• What I read this week: in Recently read (or from Today), pick your favorites, add a line about each, then post the roundup to your site, email it or copy it.
