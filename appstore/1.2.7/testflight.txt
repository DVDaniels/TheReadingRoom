Thanks for testing The Reading Room! This build (1.2.7) adds YouTube video descriptions. It has everything from 1.2.6 too.

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTIONS
• Open a YouTube video from a channel you follow: its description now shows under the player, with its paragraphs and line breaks.
• Web addresses in the description are links. Tap one to open it.
• Tap a chapter time (like 1:23) to play the video from that point.
• Videos already in the app get their description at the next refresh.

Please tell us if a description looks wrong, or if tapping a chapter time doesn't start the video where it should (Mac and iPhone/iPad).
