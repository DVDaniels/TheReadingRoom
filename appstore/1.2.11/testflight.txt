Version 1.2.11 (test build). This build lets you post from The Reading Room Plus to Tumblr, Micro.blog, Pika and other IndieWeb sites, and tells writers when you reply. It also has the first run for new readers and the movable note.

NEW: POST TO TUMBLR, MICRO.BLOG AND PIKA (PLUS)
No WordPress site? Settings › Connections now connects:
- Tumblr: press Connect Tumblr, sign in on Tumblr's page, approve, and paste the code the next page shows.
- Micro.blog, Pika or another IndieWeb site: type your site's address and press Continue, or paste an app token from your site's settings.
The Share menu's note item is now "Write a note…".

NEW: A TUMBLR TAB (PLUS)
Tumblr has its own tab under Writing. Write a note or a post (photo, tags), then Post now, Save as draft, Add to queue or Schedule it. See Your numbers, the people talking to you, and your Posts, Drafts and Queue (open, post a draft now, or delete).
Writing is for your own site. Have Tumblr too? Tick "Also post to Tumblr" in Writing or the note sheet to send the full post there as well.

NEW: A FLICKR TAB
Settings › Connections › Connect Flickr, approve on Flickr, paste the code. See your contacts' photos (friends-only too), comments, and your photos. Tap a photo to open it here: fave, comment, reply, edit your own. Tap a name to Follow them as a feed. Plus: upload, or pick a Flickr photo for a note. Comments also show in Conversations › You.

NEW: TUMBLR IN CONVERSATIONS
Replies, reblogs with words, mentions and asks from Tumblr show in Conversations › You, with a Tumblr chip. Reply on Tumblr opens your browser.

NEW: REPLIES TELL THE WRITER
When you reply to or repost a post from your own site, the app now tells the writer's site itself (a Webmention), and says whether their site was told.

FIXED: YOUR BLOG NUMBERS
If your blog's numbers can't load, the panel says why and offers Try again, instead of going blank.

ALSO IN 1.2.10
- A first run for new readers: pick circles of writers who answer each other (Help › Start with a few people › Pick writers by topic).
- Move your note out of the way: drag it by its top bar while a video plays; Tuck away / Open.
- Notes are kept as you type until you post or save them.
- Writing's Notes, Posts and Weeknotes folders each have a button to write a new one.
- Today's big photo isn't cut off in portrait any more: a fuller frame on iPad and iPhone, and tall photos are shown whole.
- Writing is now your notebook: what you're writing, ideas, drafts and this week. Your site has its own tab under it (WordPress, or Micro.blog, Pika or your site's name) with your numbers, drafts, posts and categories.
- Your site's categories fold away under On your site in that tab. Tap the heading to open it.
- Trending, under Explore: topics two or more of your feeds wrote about today or this week, in all your feeds or one folder, with Rising when more feeds than usual are on it. Today has a Trending in your feeds card: the top five today or this week, who wrote about each, how many feeds, and how it built up over the days.
- The Trending page has less text: the same chart as Today's card at the top (tap a row to jump to it), then a card per topic with a photo, the writers' faces and a small chart. Tap Headlines to see the stories.
- A story opened from Trending takes the whole window, with a Back button, and the up and down arrows go through Trending's stories.
- Plus shows once, on the Write heading in the sidebar.

PLEASE TRY
- Connect Tumblr, or your Micro.blog or Pika site. Post a note, a post with a photo, and a draft. Keep writing the draft, then Post now.
- In the Tumblr tab: add a post to your queue, schedule one, and delete a draft. Does Conversations › You show your Tumblr replies?
- Reply to a post from your site. Did the app say whether their site was told?
- Does anything look cramped or cut off on your iPhone, iPad or Mac?
