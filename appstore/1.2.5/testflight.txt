Thanks for testing The Reading Room! New in this build: more ways to find, follow and talk with the people you read in Conversations.

BRING YOUR PEOPLE OVER
• With Mastodon or Bluesky connected, the Neighborhood offers the blogs of the people you follow there. Try Follow all, or Choose to pick.

THEIR BLOGROLL
• On someone's page (tap their face or name), Their blogroll lists every site they recommend, with Follow, or Follow all.

REPOST
• Repost next to Like puts the post on your site as a short note with its picture card. Add a few words if you like. Check it on your site, and that the button turns into Reposted.

COMMENTS
• Tap "12 comments" on a post to read the comments right in the app, replies tucked under the comment they answer. Join in on their site opens their comment form.

GROUPS
• On someone's page, press Groups to put them in a group of your own, like Photographers. Your groups show as chips at the top of the Neighborhood. Rename or delete from the bar that appears. Check groups reach your other devices.

TOPICS
• Posts show the topics their writers gave them. Tap one to see everyone's posts on it, or Mute this topic. The Topics chip lists this fortnight's topics. Tags appear on new posts after a refresh.

YOUR REPLIES
• Replies you post from your site are listed in You, Your replies. When the other person writes back on their blog, it says they answered, and "Since you were here" tells you too.

DRAFTS
• Start a reply, then tap away. Your words are still there when you come back, and the post's Reply button says draft. Cancel throws the draft away.

MENTIONS
• In a reply from your site, type @ and a few letters of someone you read, then pick them. Their name links to their site, so they're told they were mentioned.

MARK ALL AS READ
• Conversations in the sidebar now shows how many new posts and replies are waiting. Mark all as read sits at the bottom of the Neighborhood.

ALSO IN THIS BETA
• Close friends: pin someone's new posts to the top of the Neighborhood, with an alert when they post.
• Liked by: posts the people you read liked on their own sites.
• Quote in a reply: select words in a post, then Quote in a reply.
• Filters: Photos, Long reads, One from each.
• Reads you: writers with you on their blogroll.
• Alerts for replies, comments and mentions (switch in the You tab).
• A clear Back button on a person's page (Esc on the Mac).
• Fixed: your emails no longer show up on Today or in the Morning Edition. They stay in Email.
• Fixed: every feed from your own site (like a Notes feed) counts as you in Conversations: your picture, the You tag, and no face for it in the row of faces.
• The same post on Mastodon and Bluesky shows once in Conversations, with "Also on Mastodon" (or Bluesky) to open the other copy. In Following, a link someone shared on both counts them once.

Something not right? Send feedback from TestFlight or open an issue at github.com/DVDaniels/TheReadingRoom/issues.
