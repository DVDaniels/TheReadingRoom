Thanks for testing The Reading Room! New in this build: Search in Conversations, and more ways to find, follow and talk with the people you read.

HELP AND FEEDBACK
• Settings, Help and feedback: Send feedback (a GitHub form with your version filled in), Rate The Reading Room, Help on the web, and the version number.
• Tap a small label on a Conversations post (the star, You, Reads you, Links to you, Back after) and it says what it means. The hearts and speech bubbles under a post say whose reactions they are when opened.

SEARCH
• Conversations, Search: when something big happens (an outage, the news), type it in and see what people are saying right now on Bluesky and Mastodon, plus posts from the blogs you read. Recent searches stay as chips. Check again looks again.

BRING YOUR PEOPLE OVER
• With Mastodon or Bluesky connected, the Neighborhood offers the blogs of the people you follow there. Try Follow all, or Choose.

A PAGE FOR EACH PERSON
• Tap a face or name. Their page now says who they are, in their own words, and lists Their blogroll with Follow or Follow all.

REPOST AND COMMENTS
• Repost next to Like puts the post on your site as a short note with its picture card. Check the button turns into Reposted.
• Tap "12 comments" to read a post's comments in the app. Join in on their site opens their comment form.

GROUPS AND TOPICS
• On someone's page, Groups puts them in a group of your own; groups show as chips in the Neighborhood and reach your other devices.
• Posts show their writers' topics. Tap one to see everyone's posts on it, or Mute this topic.

YOUR REPLIES, DRAFTS, MENTIONS
• Replies you post from your site are listed in You, Your replies, and you're told when they answer on their blog.
• An unfinished reply waits for you (the Reply button says draft). Cancel throws it away.
• In a reply from your site, type @ and pick someone you read. Their name links to their site.

MARK ALL AS READ
• Conversations in the sidebar shows how many new posts and replies are waiting. Mark all as read is at the bottom of the Neighborhood.

CHOOSE WHAT'S IN THE SIDEBAR
• Settings, Appearance, Show in the sidebar: switch off Morning Edition, Email, Help Wanted or Books if you don't use them. Each of those pages also has Hide from sidebar under its title. Nothing is deleted, and the choice syncs.

ALSO IN THIS BETA

• Quote in a reply: select words in a post, then Quote in a reply.
• Filters: Photos, Long reads, One from each. Reads you: writers with you on their blogroll.
