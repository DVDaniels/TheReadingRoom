## Description (4000)

The Reading Room is a calm, private place to read the websites, blogs and news you follow.

Add any site and it finds the feed for you. Organize feeds into folders, see what's new in Today, and read stories in a clean, distraction-free reader, right inside the app. On a big screen, your stories and the article you're reading sit side by side.

READ YOUR WAY
• Today, All stories, and a view for each folder and feed
• A clean reader that shows just the article, with its pictures
• View the original page in the app's own web window
• Keyboard shortcuts for moving fast: j and k, o to open, s to save
• Light and dark appearance that follows your Mac

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Read later for stories you want to come back to
• Highlight passages and add notes; Annotated collects them all
• Recently read, so nothing gets lost

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS or Atom feed
• Type a few words to follow that topic as a news search
• Bring your subscriptions from another reader with OPML, folders included

SHARE
• Post a story to X or LinkedIn, copy its link, or email it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your feeds, folders, highlights and notes are stored only on your Mac. The app talks directly to the sites you follow, and to nothing else.

## App Review notes

The app is an RSS and Atom feed reader. It needs no account or sign-in. To try it: open the app; it starts with a few sample feeds and refreshes them automatically. Click any story to read it in the built-in reader. Select text in an article to highlight it.
Sharing to X or LinkedIn opens those services' own share pages; signing in there is optional and only for sharing.

## App Privacy answers (the "nutrition label")

- Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? **No, we do not collect data from this app.**
- The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no account. Everything it stores stays on the Mac.

## Age rating

The app shows web pages and articles from sites the user chooses, so in the questionnaire answer **Yes** to
unrestricted web access. Apple will set a higher age rating because of that; this is normal for readers and browsers.
