Read the blogs of real people as a calm social feed. Follow writers you like, watch them answer each other, and write back to them yourself. No algorithm, no ads, no account. Reading is free.

PEOPLE FIRST
• Today: a morning briefing with the weather, what's new and what several of your writers are talking about
• People: just the individuals writing on their own sites, apart from the big publications
• Discover: blogs your writers recommend, and a search for small personal sites on any topic

CONVERSATIONS, MADE OF BLOGS
• The Neighborhood: the people you read as a social feed with their faces, no account needed
• When writers answer each other, the replies gather under the original post
• A page for each person: their posts, who they talk with and their blogroll
• Like and reply in the post; see Mastodon and Bluesky beside the blogs you read

EMAIL FROM PEOPLE, FIRST
• Connect one address of your own. People come first; newsletters wait to one side
• Pictures from the web load only when you say
• Reply all, Forward, attachments, Archive, Unsubscribe, folders, and search your whole inbox
• Follow a newsletter as a feed instead
• Works with IONOS, Gmail, iCloud Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo and most providers, and Proton Mail through Proton Mail Bridge

WRITING, WITH THE READING ROOM PLUS
• Notes, full Posts and Weeknotes for your own WordPress site, on a quiet notebook page; post now or save a draft there
• Your ideas, your site's drafts, a writing calendar and Focus
• Copies to Mastodon and Bluesky that link back home, quote cards and a weekly list of what you read
• Your blog's visitors, RSS readers and search traffic, with the free WordPress plugin The Reading Room stats

MORE TO READ AND WATCH
• Videos: channels your people share, Up next on a big screen, and a mini player that keeps playing while you read
• Podcasts saved for offline, free classics from Standard Ebooks and audiobooks from LibriVox
• The Morning Edition: yesterday's reading as a newspaper front page
• Help Wanted: job listings laid out like the classifieds
• My Day (experimental): your calendar, reminders, stocks and coins. Turn the extras off and it's simply your feed reader

READ YOUR WAY
• A clean reader with just the article and its pictures
• Listen to any story read aloud
• Text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark
• Keyboard shortcuts: j and k to move, o to open, s to save
• Read later, highlights and notes to export, rules and alerts, and widgets

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS, Atom or JSON feed
• Follow a topic, YouTube channels and podcasts, or read newsletters here
• Bring your subscriptions over with OPML, folders included

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
On iPhone and iPad too. Sync keeps your feeds, Read later, highlights and notes the same everywhere, through your own iCloud Drive.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your devices. The app talks directly to the sites, services and mail server you choose.

THE READING ROOM PLUS
Reading stays free. Plus is an optional yearly subscription for writing to your own site, with a free first week. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account's Subscriptions.
