Thanks for testing The Reading Room! This is a test build of 1.2.9, on top of everything in 1.2.8.

PLUS (new)
• Writing, posting to your own site and Your blog are now part of The Reading Room Plus, with a free first week. Reading stays free.
• In TestFlight, buying Plus costs nothing: it's Apple's test mode, and the test subscription runs out quickly.
• If your site was already connected, posting stays free for you for three months. Plus is in Settings › Connections.

MY DAY
• Wider on big screens, no more Big 3, and My Day settings in one place at the top.

WRITING (new, under Read in the sidebar)
• Everything you write for your own site in one place: a new Note, a full Post (title, featured image, category) or Weeknotes. Save it as a draft on your site or post it now, with copies to Bluesky and Mastodon.
• Ideas to write about, your drafts on your site (Keep writing opens them right here), This week (Weeknotes and What I read), and a writing calendar.
• Folders for Ideas, Drafts, Posts, Notes, Weeknotes and your categories. Delete a draft (with Undo); drafts deleted on your site leave the app too.
• Preview a post before it's on your site. A draft can become a Note, Post or Weeknotes.
• A new look: you write on a ruled notebook page, with a word count, and Focus to hide everything else (Esc or Done to come back).
• Writing in the sidebar always gives you a blank page. Anything you'd written is saved as a draft on your site first.
• One blank line between paragraphs, like on your site.
• Weeknotes no longer needs My Day: it starts from your week's posts and reading. Saving keeps it on the page; Weeknotes or Keep writing brings back the same draft. This week walks you through it in three steps: pick what you read, write, publish.

YOUR BLOG (if you connect your own WordPress site)
• Your visitors, RSS readers and search traffic, now in Writing (no longer on My Day). It needs the free WordPress plugin The Reading Room stats, from readingroomapp.com/plugin. Counting starts the day you turn the plugin on.

EMAIL
• Reads every kind of email properly: photos sent in it, every part of it, and attached files (Open or Save).
• People and Newsletters, Unsubscribe, conversations, Reply all, Forward, Archive (or swipe left on iPhone), Mark all read, Show older emails.
• Pictures from the web wait for Show pictures, so senders can't tell when you read.

ALSO NEW
• Your posts show their featured images. A tidier sidebar.

VIDEOS
• New to Videos? Channels to start with: follow iJustine or Chris Pirillo in one tap.

FIXES
• Books opens again on iPhone and Mac.
• Discover's search box searches topics again.

ALSO
• The Mac app now needs macOS 13 Ventura or later.

Please try:
• Open Plus (Writing, or Settings › Connections), try it free, then Restore purchases.
• In Writing, save a post as a draft, open it again with Keep writing, then post it. Check it lands on your site with its category and picture.
• Preview a draft, switch an old draft to Note, and tap a day on the calendar.
• Open each folder under Writing and check the lists match your site. Delete a draft, then Undo.
• In Email: open a newsletter, a reply with photos and one with a file; try Reply all, Archive and Unsubscribe.
• Follow the three steps in Writing › This week, in any order: your picks end up under What I read.
