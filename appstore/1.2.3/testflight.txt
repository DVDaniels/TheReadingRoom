Thanks for testing The Reading Room! New in this build:

EMAIL
Settings › Email lets you connect one email address of your own (a mailbox on your own domain works best: IONOS, Fastmail, iCloud, Proton through Proton Mail Bridge on the Mac, and others). Please try:
• Connecting your mailbox and checking for new mail.
• Reading an email, including newsletters with pictures and tables.
• Replying, writing a new email, and "Write back" to a writer from any story.
• Deleting an email (it goes to your mailbox's Trash).
• Each device has its own mailbox: disconnecting on one shouldn't affect another.

FOLDERS INSIDE FOLDERS
You can now put a folder inside another folder (two levels), for example Blogroll › Charlotte NC.
• Make a new folder and choose a folder to put it in, or drag a folder onto another.
• Open the outer folder: it should show stories from everything inside it.
• Check that your folders look the same on your other devices.

POSTS THAT NEED A SUBSCRIPTION
Stories from sites that usually need a subscription (and newsletter posts for paying members) now show a small amber lock.
• Look for the lock on posts from sites like the New York Times or a paid Substack.
• Open one of those feeds and press "I subscribe to this site": the lock goes away for that site.
• Try "Free to read" next to All and Unread to hide posts that need a subscription.

NOTES TO X
When you post a note, tick "Then open X with a copy". After the note posts, X opens with your words and the link to your note; press Post there. It's free and needs no X setup in the app.

Anything odd, confusing or broken: tell us at readingroomapp.com/support or reply to the TestFlight email. Screenshots help a lot.
