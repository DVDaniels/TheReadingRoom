Version 1.2.10 (test build). Version 1.2.9 is waiting for App Store review; this build adds a first run for new readers and a fix for notes.

NEW: A FIRST RUN FOR NEW READERS
Someone opening The Reading Room for the first time, with no feeds yet, now gets a short welcome instead of an empty app.
- Welcome: real people are still writing on the web, and they're answering each other.
- Pick what you like: five small circles of writers who read and answer each other (the web and design, the small web, tech, Apple and the Mac, books and ideas). Pick one or more and see who's in them.
- Follow them in one tap, then see your first conversation: posts from those writers that answer one another.
- Already have feeds? "I already have feeds to add" skips straight to adding them.
- To try it with the feeds you already have: Help (New to RSS? How to use this app) › Start with a few people › Pick writers by topic.

FIXED: NOTES DON'T GET LOST
A note you're writing about a post or video is now kept on your device as you type. Close it (or tap outside it) to rewind the video, open Note again, and it's all still there, with "Start over" if you want a blank one. It's cleared once you post it or save it as a draft.

PLEASE TRY
- Pick writers by topic, choose a circle or two, and follow them. Did a first conversation show up? Did it make sense?
- Start a note about a video, close it, rewind, and open Note again. Is your note still there?
- Does anything look cramped or cut off on your iPhone, iPad or Mac?
