Thanks for testing The Reading Room! New in this build:

FOLLOW A SITE FROM ITS OWN PAGE
Sites can now offer an "Open in The Reading Room" button, and feed:// links open in the app too. Please try:
• Go to readingroomapp.com/button, type the address of any blog you like and press "Make my button". Press the green button it shows, then "Open in The Reading Room". The app should come to the front and ask "Follow (that site)?" Press Follow and check the feed is added.
• Try it with the app closed and with it already open.
• Press "Not now" and check nothing is added.
• On iPhone, try the same in Safari.
• If you have a feed:// link handy, click it: the app should offer to follow that feed.

THEN OPEN X WITH A COPY
When you post a note, a new tick box can open X's own post window with your words and the link, ready for you to post.

NOTES SHOW THE POST'S PICTURE
A note about a post (Share › Note on your site) now ends with a card: the post's picture, title and site, linking to it. The picture also becomes the note's featured image, unless you add your own photo. Try it on a post with a picture and one without.

A MORE VISUAL MORNING EDITION
The Morning Edition has more pictures and colour: a row of the people in today's paper, pictures on more stories, an "In pictures" strip, and colour-coded In brief sections. Open it in light and dark mode, and tell us if anything looks off or a picture doesn't show.

PRINTING WORKS ON MAC
Print on the Morning Edition used to say "This application does not support printing." Press Print and check the print window opens, the preview looks like the front page, and it prints or saves as a PDF.

CONVERSATIONS: A SOCIAL FEED MADE OF BLOGS
Conversations now has three tabs: Neighborhood, Following and You. No account is needed for the Neighborhood. Please try:

The Neighborhood
• The posts of the people you read, like a social feed, with their faces (their Bluesky or Mastodon picture, or their blog's icon; these fill in after the app checks each site, so give it a minute the first time).
• "Since you were here" at the top sums up what's new. Tap a conversation in it to open the post.
• When writers reply to each other, the replies show under the original post as a thread. These fill in as your feeds refresh, so give it a refresh or two.
• With Mastodon or Bluesky connected: short posts there by writers you read show up under their face, and blog posts show who shared them.
• "Writers your writers link to": blogs that several people you read link to. Try Follow, and the x to stop suggesting one.
• Scroll to the end: it should say you're caught up.

A page for each person
• Tap a face or a name. You should see their posts, who they talk with, emails from them, blogs they link to, and their blogroll. "Their page" on a person's feed opens it too.

Like and reply
• With your WordPress site connected, Like on a blog post puts it on a Likes page on your site (made the first time; it asks first) and tells their site. Like again to take it back.
• Like works on Bluesky and Mastodon posts too. On Mastodon you may be asked to disconnect and connect again once.
• Reply opens a box right in the card: post a reply on your site, or email them.
• Some posts show likes and replies from other sites (for example "8 likes, 2 replies"). Tap to see who.

You
• Replies on Mastodon and Bluesky, comments and Webmentions on your own site, blogs linking to you, and emails from people you read, in one list. The buttons above it show just one kind.

Reading a post from Conversations
• It now takes the whole window, with the Conversations button to go back to the same place.
• Under the post: answers from blogs you read, who shared it, likes from other sites, and Like / Reply.

Everything from 1.2.3 (email, folders inside folders, subscription locks) is in this build too.

Anything odd, confusing or broken: tell us at readingroomapp.com/support or reply to the TestFlight email. Screenshots help a lot.
