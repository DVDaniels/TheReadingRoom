The Reading Room is a calm, private place to read the web, written by people.

Follow the blogs, writers and news you choose. There's no algorithm deciding what you see and nothing pushed in because it gets clicks: every post from the sites you follow, newest first, in a clean reader made for reading.

PEOPLE FIRST
• Today: a morning briefing with the time, the weather and what's new, with people's posts up front
• People: just the individuals writing on their own sites, separate from the big publications
• Discover: blogs recommended by the writers you already read, plus a search that finds news and small personal sites on any topic

CONVERSATIONS, MADE OF BLOGS
• The Neighborhood: the posts of the people you read, as a social feed with their faces, no account needed
• When writers answer each other on their blogs, the replies gather under the original post as a thread
• A page for each person: their posts, who they talk with, the blogs they link to and their blogroll
• Like and reply right in the post; with your WordPress site connected, likes go on a Likes page on your site
• Connect Mastodon or Bluesky to see their posts there too, and hide, block or report spam accounts

EMAIL, FOR WRITING BACK
• Before social media, people with websites found each other by email. Connect one address of your own and Write back sends your note from the app; their answer lands beside their posts
• Works with IONOS, Gmail, iCloud Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo and most providers

NOTES ON YOUR OWN SITE
• Post a quick thought, with a photo and a link, to your own WordPress site first, then share copies to Mastodon and Bluesky that link back home. A note about a post shows its picture

BOOKS AND AUDIOBOOKS
• Free classics from Standard Ebooks and free audiobooks from LibriVox, with a daily reading goal if you'd like one

HELP WANTED
• Job listings from well-known boards, laid out like the classifieds; circle the ones you like and track them

THE MORNING EDITION
• Yesterday's reading laid out as a newspaper front page with pictures, made fresh at 6 every morning, ready to print
• Your year in reading: stories read, books finished, your longest run of reading days and the people you read most

NEWSLETTERS, ALERTS AND MORE
• Read newsletters here instead of your inbox, with a private address of your own
• Rules & alerts for words you care about, and Watch a page that has no feed
• Feed checkup finds feeds that broke or went quiet
• Save any page from the Share menu, and widgets for your latest stories and your reading streak

READ YOUR WAY
• A clean reader that shows just the article, with its pictures, side by side with your list on iPad
• Listen to any story read aloud, with your choice of voice
• Text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark for the whole app
• Pull to refresh, swipe a story to save it, swipe from the edge to go back

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Read later, highlights and notes ready to export, and Recently read

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS, Atom or JSON feed
• Follow a topic as a news search, or YouTube channels and podcasts
• Bring your subscriptions from another reader with OPML, folders included, and put folders inside folders
• A small lock marks posts that need a subscription
• Mute words you're tired of

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• The Reading Room is on the Mac too. Turn on sync and your feeds, folders, Read later, highlights, books and notes stay the same everywhere, through a folder in your own iCloud Drive. Your sign-ins follow through iCloud Keychain.

NEW TO RSS?
A short guide inside the app explains how feeds work, how to start your own site, and suggests a few good people to follow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your devices, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on sync. The app talks directly to the sites, services and mail server you choose.
