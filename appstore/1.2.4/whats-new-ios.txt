• Email, for writing to the people you read. Before social media, people with websites found each other by email. Connect one address of your own and Write back sends your note right from the app; their answer lands beside their posts. Works with IONOS, Gmail, iCloud Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo and most providers.
• Conversations is now a social feed made of blogs. The Neighborhood shows the posts of the people you read with their faces, no account needed. When writers answer each other on their blogs, the replies gather under the original post.
• A page for each person: their posts, who they talk with, the blogs they link to and their blogroll.
• Like and reply right in the post. With your WordPress site connected, Like adds the post to a Likes page on your site and lets the writer know.
• You: replies, comments on your site and blogs linking to you, in one list.
• The Morning Edition: yesterday's reading laid out like a newspaper front page, with pictures, made fresh at 6 every morning.
• Your year in reading, and widgets for your latest stories and your reading streak.
• Save from Safari with "Reading Room" in the Share menu.
• Newsletters out of your inbox, Rules & alerts, Watch a page, and Feed checkup.
• Folders inside folders.
• A small lock on posts that need a subscription, and Free to read to hide them.
• Follow a site from its own page: Open in The Reading Room buttons and feed:// links open the app.
• Notes show the post's picture, and can open X with a copy for you to post.
• Light, sepia or dark for the whole app, a tidier sidebar and Settings in one place.
