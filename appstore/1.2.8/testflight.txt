Thanks for testing The Reading Room! This build (1.2.8) is small, on top of everything from 1.2.7.

UNFOLLOW FROM SOMEONE'S PAGE
• Open a writer's page (tap their name in Conversations). Unfollow is now at the end of the row of buttons. It asks first, then removes all of that person's feeds, takes them off Close friends, and takes you back to where you were.

ONE CARD PER POST
• Something you posted to both Mastodon and Bluesky now shows once in Conversations › You › Your posts, with both icons, the replies on each, and Open on Mastodon / Open on Bluesky.

BIRTHDAYS ON MY DAY
• The cake only shows for real birthdays: the Birthdays calendar from Contacts, or an event with "birthday" in its name. Other events no longer count.

STARTER WRITERS
• The writers the app suggests when you're just starting out have changed a little.

Please try:
• Unfollow someone from their page, then check they're gone from the Neighborhood and the sidebar.
• Unfollow someone who was a close friend.
• Post a link to both Mastodon and Bluesky and check Your posts shows it once.

Please tell us if anything about unfollowing surprises you.
