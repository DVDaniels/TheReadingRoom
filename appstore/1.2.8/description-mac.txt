Read the blogs of real people as a calm social feed. Follow writers you like, watch them answer each other, and write back to them yourself. No algorithm, no ads, no account.

Every post from the sites you follow arrives newest first, in a clean reader made for reading.

PEOPLE FIRST
• Today: a morning briefing with the time, the weather, what's new and what several of your writers are talking about, with people's posts up front
• People: just the individuals writing on their own sites, separate from the big publications
• Discover: blogs recommended by the writers you already read, plus a search that finds news and small personal sites on any topic

CONVERSATIONS, MADE OF BLOGS
• The Neighborhood: the posts of the people you read, as a social feed with their faces, no account needed
• When writers answer each other on their blogs, the replies gather under the original post as a thread
• A page for each person: their posts, who they talk with, the blogs they link to and their blogroll
• Like and reply right in the post, and keep your likes on your own site
• Search what people say about any topic on Mastodon and Bluesky, beside the blogs you read
• Connect Mastodon or Bluesky to see their posts there too

EMAIL, FOR WRITING BACK
• Connect one address of your own and Write back sends your note from the app; their answer lands beside their posts
• Works with IONOS, Gmail, iCloud Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo and most providers, and Proton Mail on a Mac through Proton Mail Bridge

NOTES ON YOUR OWN SITE
• Post a quick thought, with a photo and a link, to your own WordPress site first, then share copies to Mastodon and Bluesky that link back home
• Quote cards from passages you highlight, and a weekly list of what you read

MY DAY (EXPERIMENTAL)
• Start the day with your next two weeks and the month from Calendar, your Big 3, your reminders, and a few stocks and coins with trend lines
• A Sunday Weekly Preview that ends with a private page for Day One and Weeknotes for your blog
• Turn the extras off in Settings and it's simply your feed reader

BOOKS AND AUDIOBOOKS
• Free classics from Standard Ebooks and free audiobooks from LibriVox, with a daily reading goal

HELP WANTED
• Job listings laid out like the classifieds; circle the ones you like and track them

THE MORNING EDITION
• Yesterday's reading as a newspaper front page each morning, ready to print
• Your year in reading: stories, books and the people you read most

NEWSLETTERS, ALERTS AND MORE
• Read newsletters here instead of your inbox, with a private address of your own
• Rules & alerts for words you care about
• Save any page from the Share menu, and widgets

VIDEOS AND PODCASTS
• A Videos tab, channels your people share, a mini player that keeps playing while you read, and podcasts saved for offline

READ YOUR WAY
• A clean reader that shows just the article, with its pictures, side by side with your list on a big screen
• Listen to any story read aloud, with your choice of voice
• Text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark
• Keyboard shortcuts: j and k to move, o to open the original, s to save

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Read later, highlights and notes ready to export, and Recently read

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS, Atom or JSON feed
• Follow a topic as a news search, or YouTube channels and podcasts
• Bring your subscriptions from another reader with OPML, folders included, and put folders inside folders
• Mute words you're tired of

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• The Reading Room is on iPhone and iPad too. Turn on sync and your feeds, folders, Read later, highlights, books and notes stay the same everywhere, through a folder in your own iCloud Drive.

NEW TO RSS?
A short guide explains feeds and suggests good people to follow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your devices, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on sync. The app talks directly to the sites, services and mail server you choose.
