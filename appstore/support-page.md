# The Reading Room

A calm, private RSS reader for Mac, iPhone and iPad, made by David Daniels. *Written by people.*

The Reading Room follows the websites, blogs and news you choose and shows them in one place: organized into folders, readable in a clean built-in reader, with highlights and notes that stay on your devices. No account, no tracking, no ads.

## New to RSS?

Almost every blog and news site publishes a feed, a simple list of its newest posts. The Reading Room checks the feeds you choose and shows you what's new, in order, with no algorithm in between. Inside the app, **New to RSS?** at the bottom of the sidebar (or **Help › The Reading Room Guide** on the Mac) walks you through it and suggests a few good people to follow.

## Getting started

- **Add a feed:** press ⌘N, then paste a website address. The app finds its feed. Or type a few words, like *pharmacy news*, to follow that topic.
- **Make folders:** click the folder button next to *Feeds*, then drag feeds onto a folder.
- **Coming from another reader?** Export your subscriptions as an OPML file there, then choose **Feeds › Import OPML…**. Folders come along.

## Reading

- Click a story to read it in the app. **View original** opens the full web page in the app's own window.
- Select text in an article and choose **Highlight** or **Add note**. Find them all under **Annotated**.
- Keyboard: `j` / `k` next and previous, `o` view original, `s` read later, `m` read / unread, ⌘R refresh.

## Common questions

**Some stories only show a summary.** A few sites keep their articles behind a paywall or build their pages with scripts the reader can't use. Choose **View original** to read those on the site.

**Where is my information stored?** Only on your devices, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on sync. See the [privacy policy](/reading-room/privacy/).

**How do I move my feeds to another Mac?** Choose **Feeds › Export OPML…** on the old Mac and **Import OPML…** on the new one.
