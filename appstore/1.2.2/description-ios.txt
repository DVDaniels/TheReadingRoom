The Reading Room is a calm, private place to read the web, written by people.

Follow the blogs, writers and news you choose. There's no algorithm deciding what you see and nothing pushed in because it gets clicks: every post from the sites you follow, newest first, in a clean reader made for reading.

PEOPLE FIRST
• Today: a morning briefing with the time, the weather and what's new, with people's posts up front
• People: just the individuals writing on their own sites, separate from the big publications
• Discover: blogs recommended by the writers you already read, plus a search that finds news and small personal sites on any topic

CONVERSATIONS
• Connect your Mastodon or Bluesky account to see what the people you follow are sharing and read whole reply threads
• Answer right from the app, follow the people you read, and find the blogs of the people you follow
• Hide, block or report spam accounts

NOTES ON YOUR OWN SITE
• Post a quick thought, with a photo and a link, to your own WordPress site first, then share copies to Mastodon and Bluesky that link back home

BOOKS AND AUDIOBOOKS
• Free classics from Standard Ebooks, read a page at a time with your choice of text size
• Free audiobooks from LibriVox, with chapters and speed control
• A daily reading goal and streak, if you'd like one

HELP WANTED
• Job listings from well-known boards, laid out like the classifieds, with your own local search
• Circle the ones you like and track them from circled to applied to offer

THE MORNING EDITION
• Yesterday's reading laid out as a newspaper front page, made fresh at 6 every morning, ready to print
• Your year in reading: stories read, books finished, your longest run of reading days and the people you read most

NEWSLETTERS, ALERTS AND MORE
• Read newsletters here instead of your inbox, with a private address of your own
• Rules & alerts: a notification when a story mentions words you care about, or save and skip stories automatically
• Watch a page that has no feed, and hear when it changes
• Search everything you've read, including older stories
• Feed checkup finds feeds that broke, went quiet or that you never open
• Save any page from the Share menu, and widgets for your latest stories and your reading streak

READ YOUR WAY
• A clean reader that shows just the article, with its pictures, side by side with your list on iPad
• Listen to any story read aloud, with your choice of voice
• Text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark
• Translate any story into your language
• Pull to refresh, swipe a story to save it, swipe from the edge to go back

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Read later for stories you want to come back to
• Highlight passages and add notes; Annotated collects them all, ready to export
• Recently read, so nothing gets lost

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS, Atom or JSON feed
• Type a few words to follow that topic as a news search
• Follow people on Mastodon and Bluesky, YouTube channels and podcasts, too
• Bring your subscriptions from another reader with OPML, folders included
• Mute words you're tired of and hide websites from your news searches

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• The Reading Room is on the Mac too. Turn on sync and your feeds, folders, Read later, highlights, books and notes stay the same everywhere, through a folder in your own iCloud Drive. Your sign-ins follow through iCloud Keychain.

NEW TO RSS?
A short guide inside the app explains how feeds work, how to start your own site, and suggests a few good people to follow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your devices, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on sync. The app talks directly to the sites and services you choose.
