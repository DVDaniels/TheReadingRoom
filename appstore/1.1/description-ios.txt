The Reading Room is a calm, private place to read the web, written by people.

Follow the blogs, writers and news you choose. There's no algorithm deciding what you see and nothing pushed in because it gets clicks: every post from the sites you follow, newest first, in a clean reader made for reading.

PEOPLE FIRST
• People: just the individuals writing on their own sites, separate from the big publications
• Today: a morning briefing with the time, the weather and what's new, with people's posts up front
• Discover: blogs recommended by the writers you already read, through their blogrolls and links

READ YOUR WAY
• A clean reader that shows just the article, with its pictures, side by side with your list on iPad
• Listen to any story read aloud, with your choice of voice
• Text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark
• Pull to refresh, swipe a story to save it, swipe from the edge to go back

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Read later for stories you want to come back to
• Highlight passages and add notes; Annotated collects them all, ready to export
• Recently read, so nothing gets lost

FOLLOW ANYTHING
• Paste a website and The Reading Room finds its RSS, Atom or JSON feed
• Type a few words to follow that topic as a news search
• Bring your subscriptions from another reader with OPML, folders included
• Mute words you're tired of and hide websites from your news searches

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• The Reading Room is on the Mac too. Turn on sync and your feeds, folders, Read later, highlights and notes stay the same everywhere, through a folder in your own iCloud Drive

NEW TO RSS?
A short guide inside the app explains how feeds work and suggests a few good people to follow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your devices, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on sync. The app talks directly to the sites you follow.
