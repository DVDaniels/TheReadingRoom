Written by people. This update is about reading real writers, not whatever gets the most clicks.

• People: a view of just the individuals you follow, writing on their own sites. On a big screen, each person gets a card with their newest post.
• Discover: blogs recommended by the writers you already read, found through their blogrolls and the links in their posts. Follow with one click.
• Today is now your morning paper: a short briefing, a clock, the local weather, and posts from people up front.
• Follow people anywhere: paste a Mastodon or Bluesky handle, a YouTube channel or an Apple Podcasts link. Videos and podcast episodes play right in the app.
• Write back: reply to a writer by email, in their comments, or on Mastodon or Bluesky, whichever they offer.
• Listen: have any story read aloud, with your choice of voice.
• Reading settings: text size, line width, and light, sepia or dark.
• Muted words, and a button to hide a website from your news searches. Both sync across your devices.
• The same story from several news searches is shown once, and Google News stories name their real source.
• New to RSS? A short guide explains how it works and suggests a few good people to follow.
• Pictures for stories that didn't come with one, and many smaller fixes.
