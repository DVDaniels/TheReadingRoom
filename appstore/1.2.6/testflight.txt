Thanks for testing The Reading Room! This build fixes replies you post to your own site, and sending email through Proton Mail Bridge.

REPLIES
• Reply to a post and choose Post on your site: the reply is now marked as a reply, so the other site sees it as one (before, it arrived as a bookmark).
• The Reply box now says whether their site shows replies from other sites. If it does, your reply can appear under their post. If it doesn't, it says the reply stays on your site, and offers their comments section when the post has one.
• Sites that announce replies only in their server headers are now recognized too.

EMAIL
• Sending through Proton Mail Bridge no longer stops with "The mail server stopped answering." The app now works with Bridge set to SSL or STARTTLS, and connecting a mailbox checks sending too.

REPOSTS
• Reposting a YouTube or Vimeo video now says "Reposted" above the player, so your site shows it as a repost instead of a plain note.
• Videos: a new Videos tab for the YouTube channels you follow and video podcasts, kept out of Today (hide it in Settings › Appearance to have them back in Today). It shows "Channels your people share": channels whose videos turned up in posts by people you read, with Follow. Under a video in a story there's "Follow <channel>", and pasting a link to any YouTube video into Add feed follows its channel.
• Save for offline: podcast episodes and video files from feeds can be saved to your device and play without a connection (finished ones are removed after a week; Settings › Saved for offline shows the space used). YouTube and Vimeo videos can't be saved.
• YouTube channels whose feed YouTube won't give out (it answers "not found" for some channels) now work: the app reads the channel's Videos page instead. Members-only videos are marked.
• "Reposted" now opens a choice: see your repost, or repost it again. If you delete a repost from your site, the button goes back to "Repost".
• A note about a YouTube or Vimeo video now shares to Bluesky with the video as its card, so it plays right in the post (the link to your note goes in the words). On Mastodon it already plays.
• Keep watching while you browse: leave a story while its video or podcast plays and it keeps going in a small box in the corner. Tap its title to go back; × stops it. Video files can go to Picture in picture, and on the Mac any video can open in its own window.
• Refreshing while you're on Videos now keeps you on Videos.
• Answer a video in its own comments: in the reply box, Comment on YouTube (or Rumble, Vimeo, Odysee, Dailymotion, TikTok) copies your words and opens the video, so you can paste them there.

Please try a reply to a blog that shows webmentions, and one that doesn't, and tell us what the Reply box said. If you use Proton, send an email from the app. Repost a video and check your site. Play a video, go back to the list, and see that it keeps playing in the corner.
