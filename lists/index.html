Written by people.

20 personal blogs about the web, writing and making things. Follow the list once, and blogs I add later show up in your reader on their own.

Download the file for readers that only import.

How to follow it

  1. Copy the address above.

  2. In The Reading Room, choose + Add a feed, then Follow a list of feeds (OPML), and paste it.

  3. Read. Every blog lands in a “Written by People” folder. The app checks the list twice a day and adds new blogs for you.

Following lists is new, in the beta for now. Other readers that support OPML subscriptions, such as FreshRSS, can follow it too.
